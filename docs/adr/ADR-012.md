# ADR-012: Detector composition

Status: Accepted

Context: all 30 OPSSAT-AD baselines, including IForest and a VAE, consume the same 18-feature vector; an IForest + AE ensemble on that identical vector is a re-run of two baselines, not an ensemble, and the rubric scores detection quality at 0%. Decision: IForest on the 18 features as primary (directly comparable to the benchmark), split conformal as the calibration layer and stated contribution. AE demoted to gated stretch on raw signals (real complementarity), trimmed training, AOM/MOA fusion. Consequences: honest claims, apples-to-apples numbers, and the writeup's story is score-to-decision, not detection lift.
